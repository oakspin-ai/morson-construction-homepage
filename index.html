From the dirt up.

Every build moves through the same three stages, and Bob runs all of them. Here is what that looks like on our job sites.

  1. Plan and permit

    Bob joins early: design reviews, material choices and value engineering, then the City approval process. Structural problems in older homes get found and fixed before they cost you.

    Foundation improvements project

  2. Bid and build

    Subcontractors walk the job, pick up plans and bid against a firm deadline. Bob holds every trade accountable and keeps phases moving without dead time between them.

    Whole-home remodel in progress

  3. Finish and move in

    The last coat of stucco gets the same attention as the foundation. One family’s first housewarming invitation went to Bob.

What we build.

Whatever the size of the job, you get the same owner-managed crew. Most of our work is in San Clemente and along the coast to Laguna Beach.

  • Custom homes

    Complete new builds from bare lot to final inspection, managed by one contractor.

  • Whole-home remodels

    Down-to-the-studs renovations, including older beach homes that need reworking.

  • Kitchens and bathrooms

    Single-room remodels get the same planning and finish quality as a full build.

  • Foundations and structural

    Foundation improvements and repairs to older construction before it becomes a bigger problem.

  • Hardscape and outdoor living

    Patios, decks and rooftop spaces built to handle the coastal climate.

  • Metal roofing

    Durable metal roof systems for new homes and re-roofs.

  • Green building

    Energy-conscious materials and methods, chosen to fit your budget.

  • Construction consulting

    Help with bids, budgets, schedules and City approvals when you’re running your own project.
